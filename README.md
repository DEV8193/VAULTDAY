# VAULTDAY
A privacy-first, local-only productivity app. Tasks, projects, subtasks, a day planner, and a karma system — all sealed on your device. No accounts. No cloud. No tracking.

Vaultday is a local-first productivity app built for people who don't want their plans living on someone else's server.

Everything — tasks, projects, notes, schedules — stays entirely on your device. No account required, no internet connection needed, no telemetry phoning home. Open the app offline on day one and it works exactly the same as day one thousand.

It ships with a day planner, calendar, kanban board, and stats view. Tasks support priorities, due dates, labels, subtasks, and recurring schedules. A karma system tracks your streaks and rewards consistency without ever punishing you for missing a day. Pre-built templates let you start structured projects — including full hypertrophy training plans with every exercise as a tappable subtask — in a single click.

Built with Electron and vanilla JS. No frameworks. No build step. Just open it and go.
