The Philosophy

Built different,
on purpose.

Most productivity apps are free because you're the product. They track what you work on, when you work, what you complete, and how long things take — then use that to train models, serve ads, or sell your data.

Vaultday makes the opposite bet. Your tasks never leave your device. There's no server to breach, no account to leak, no company reading your to-do list. Not because we're small — because it's a core design decision we're not willing to trade away.

Your plans are yours. Full stop.
